# Water

#resource #water #stage2

What a band drinks. A band without water for a day begins to thirst and
loses people faster than from anything else. The rules are in
[Band](../units/band.md#stores).

___

## Properties

| Field       | Value                                  |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| unit        | a litre                                |
| `weight_kg` | 1.0                                    |
| `spoils`    | 5 days                                 |
| gathered by | the property `water` of a cell         |
| needed      | 2 units per person per day             |

___

## Where it is found

Every biome that lists `water` among its resources. A river adds to the
water of its cell, the shallows at the shore give it freely and
dangerously, and the water of a swamp or a marsh may poison; see
[Bands](../game/01_bands.md#the-shore).
