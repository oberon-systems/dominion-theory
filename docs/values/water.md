# Water

#resource #water #stage2

What a band drinks. An adult who drank in full lives through a day
without water, thirsts the second day and dies on the third; a child
holds out less. The rules are in
[Band](../units/band.md#hunger-and-thirst).

___

## Properties

| Field       | Value                                  |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| unit        | a litre                                |
| `weight_kg` | 1.0                                    |
| `spoils`    | 5 days                                 |
| gathered by | the property `water` of a cell         |
| needed      | 1 unit per person per day              |

___

## Where it is found

Every biome that lists `water` among its resources. A river adds to the
water of its cell, the shallows at the shore give it freely and
dangerously, and the water of a swamp or a marsh may poison; see
[Bands](../game/01_bands.md#the-shore).
