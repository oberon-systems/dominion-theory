# Forest

#biome #forest

Woodland. It is the richest land of the band era, with water close by, but
it is slower to walk and hides more that can hurt. The model is in
[the overview](overview.md).

___

## Where it appears

Forest takes the wetter half of the land that is left after every biome
with a rule of its own: the water, the cold, the heights, the banks of
rivers, the desert and the shores.

| Order | Rule              |
|-------|-------------------|
| 70    | moisture from 0.5 |

___

## Band era

| Property      | Base | Recovery | Seasons                                 |
|---------------|------|----------|-----------------------------------------|
| `water`       | 0.6  | 3 days   | none                                    |
| `food`        | 0.9  | 30 days  | spring 0.7, autumn 1.2, winter 0.4      |
| `threat`      | 0.4  | none     | none                                    |
| `passability` | 0.6  | none     | none                                    |

Resources: `water`, `forage`. Colour: `#006400`.

A stripped forest takes longer to recover than grass, so a band that
stays is pushed to move on.
