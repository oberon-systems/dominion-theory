# Snow

#biome #snow

Land under snow the whole year: the caps at the poles. There is water to
melt and next to nothing else. The model is in
[the overview](overview.md).

___

## Where it appears

Snow is the coldest land of all, colder than tundra, which lies around
it as a belt.

| Order | Rule                    |
|-------|-------------------------|
| 25    | temperature up to 0.07  |

It comes after mountains, so a range at a pole is still a range, and
before tundra.
