# Grass

#biome #grass

Open land: meadow, steppe, light scrub. It is the easiest land to cross and
the safest, and it feeds a band well for most of the year. The model is in
[the overview](overview.md).

___

## Where it appears

Grass has no rule of its own. It is the land that no other biome claimed,
which makes it the most common one on warm and temperate land.

| Order | Rule |
|-------|------|
| 90    | none |
