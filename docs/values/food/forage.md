# Forage

#resource #food #stage2

The food of a band: whatever can be picked, dug up or caught by hand. A
band short of it for two days begins to starve. The rules are in
[Band](../../units/band.md#stores).

___

## Properties

| Field       | Value                                  |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| unit        | a kilo                                 |
| `weight_kg` | 1.0                                    |
| `spoils`    | 3 days                                 |
| gathered by | the property `food` of a cell          |
| needed      | 1 unit per person per day              |

___

## Where it is found

Every biome that lists `forage` among its resources. The forest and the
floodplain are the richest, the season changes what they give, and a
cell that was gathered from needs weeks to grow back.
