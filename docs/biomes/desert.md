# Desert

#biome #desert

Dry, warm land with almost nothing on it. It is easy to walk and hard to
live in; a river through it is worth more than anywhere else. The model is
in [the overview](overview.md).

___

## Where it appears

The desert is the driest 15 percent of land in the warm half of the
planet.

| Order | Rule                                      |
|-------|-------------------------------------------|
| 60    | moisture up to 0.15, temperature from 0.5 |

Dry land in the cold half stays grass or becomes tundra.

The first window is placed in the temperate north and asks for forest, so
a band does not start in a desert. It is on the planet for the eras that
see more of it.
